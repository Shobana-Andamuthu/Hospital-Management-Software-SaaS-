Support Information

Frequently asked contact questions

Quick answers to common questions about consultations, pilot sandbox setups, and technical requirements.

How quickly will an onboarding coordinator respond to our inquiry?

During normal business hours (08:00 – 20:00 IST), a clinical deployment engineer will contact you within 2 to 4 hours. Critical hospital support tickets are addressed 24/7 under our guaranteed emergency response SLA.

Can we request an on-premise demonstration at our hospital facility?

Yes. For hospitals with 50 or more licensed inpatient beds, our clinical solutions team conducts on-site operational reviews, ward hardware audits, and physician workflow walkthroughs across major metropolitan regions.

What details should we prepare before our consultation call?

It is helpful to have an approximate breakdown of your inpatient bed capacity, primary surgical and medical specialties, active laboratory analyzer models, and existing electronic billing software.
