Demo Questions

Frequently asked questions about the walkthrough

Everything you need to know before attending your CareGrid clinical session.

Is there any software installation required to participate?

No. CareGrid is 100% web-based. The demonstration takes place over a secure web conference link (Google Meet / Zoom). You can view the live clinical console from any standard web browser.

Who from our hospital should attend the session?

We recommend including your Medical Director, Nursing Superintendent, Head of IT, and Chief Financial Officer. The session is structured to address clinical, administrative, and billing questions simultaneously.

Can our team test CareGrid directly in our own hospital sandbox?

Yes. Following the initial walkthrough, we can provision a dedicated, isolated trial sandbox for your department heads to test bedside charting, bed telemetry, and rosters with realistic synthetic patients.
