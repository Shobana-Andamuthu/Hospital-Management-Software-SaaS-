Enterprise Security

Built specifically for sensitive healthcare data & regulatory compliance

Security and data sovereignty are integral architectural foundations of CareGrid, not retrofitted third-party add-ons.

All clinical records and billing databases are physically hosted within sovereign Equinix Tier IV facilities in India, compliant with national digital healthcare frameworks.

Granular Role-Based Access (RBAC)
