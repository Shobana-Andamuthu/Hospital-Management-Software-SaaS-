Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything hospital executives and medical directors ask before deploying CareGrid.

Can we upgrade our plan as our hospital expands its bed capacity?

Yes. You can scale seamlessly from Clinic to Hospital tier with zero disruption. All patient records, billing ledgers, staff rosters, and ward configurations remain preserved without needing to re-enter data.

How long does the hospital onboarding and data migration process take?

For smaller clinics and outpatient units, deployment takes under 5 business days. For acute multi-specialty hospitals (50 to 250 beds), our structured 4-week rollout ensures zero downtime to your live healthcare operations.

Are there any hidden fees or penalties for additional staff user accounts?

No. CareGrid plans are priced transparently by facility bed capacity, not by the number of user seats. You can create as many doctor, nurse, technician, and administrative accounts as your facility needs.

Does CareGrid comply with domestic healthcare data residency regulations?

Yes. All patient medical records and telemetry logs are encrypted using AES-256 and stored exclusively inside certified domestic data centers, ensuring full compliance with domestic healthcare governance mandates.

What hardware do clinicians need to access CareGrid?

CareGrid is fully cloud-native and accessible through standard modern web browsers. It runs smoothly on desktop workstations, nurse station computers, iPads, and Android clinical tablets without requiring proprietary local servers.
